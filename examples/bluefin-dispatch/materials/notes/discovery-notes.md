# Discovery notes — Bluefin Freight

Week 1–2 discovery, on site at the main depot.

## How dispatch works today

- Bluefin runs 64 trucks from 3 depots and completes about 340 deliveries per day.
- 5 dispatchers build the next day's routes every morning in three shared spreadsheets.
- Morning planning takes about 2.5 hours; trucks often leave late because routes are not final.
- Drivers get their stops as a printed sheet. Changes during the day go by phone call.
- Customers have no visibility. The office takes around 120 "where is my truck?" calls per day.

## Pain points named by the client

1. Late departures cascade into missed delivery windows (on-time rate 81% last quarter).
2. Empty miles: trucks return to the depot empty on 18% of route kilometres.
3. Dispatchers cannot see where trucks are without calling the driver.
4. Proof of delivery is paper-based; disputes take days to resolve.

## Constraints

- Must keep the existing in-cab telematics units (contract runs until 2027).
- Rural routes have poor coverage, so the driver app has to work offline.
- Go-live before the peak season in October.
