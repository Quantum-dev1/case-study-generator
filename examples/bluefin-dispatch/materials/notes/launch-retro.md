# Launch retrospective

## Timeline

- Kickoff 11 February 2025. MVP (console + driver app, manual routing) live at the first depot after 9 weeks.
- Route optimiser and customer notifications added in weeks 10–14; all 3 depots live after 14 weeks.

## What went well

- Pilot at one depot first: drivers gave feedback on the app daily, and 61 of 64 drivers were using it
  within two weeks of the all-depot rollout.
- The optimiser respects customer time windows and driver hours; dispatchers can still drag stops by hand.
- Offline mode: stops, signatures and photos are queued on the phone and synced when the signal returns.

## Numbers after the first full month (September 2025)

- Morning planning is down to about 25 minutes for the whole fleet.
- On-time delivery rate reached 94%.
- Empty-mile share fell to 11% thanks to backhaul suggestions in the optimiser.

## What we would do differently

- Start the telematics integration earlier; the vendor API documentation was incomplete and cost us a week.
